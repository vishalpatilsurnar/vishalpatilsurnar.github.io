01 — About

Hiring at volume, without dropping the ball

Most hiring problems are really process problems. A strong candidate goes cold because nobody followed up. A role sits open three weeks longer than it should because approvals stalled somewhere nobody was watching. A manager plans headcount off a number that was never checked.

My work is closing those gaps. Over nine years — across Ireland and India, in healthcare services and technology — I've run the recruitment engine behind the scenes: high-volume screening, full-cycle hiring administration, ATS and CRM hygiene, candidate communication, and the reporting hiring managers actually plan from.

At Jaunas Technology I screened 3,000+ profiles a month at 98% match accuracy and lifted candidate experience scores by 20% by renegotiating how departments handed work between them. At Justdial I held data accuracy at 99% and became the team's final check before anything went out. Today at RelateCare in Co. Waterford I run hiring administration for 200+ applicants, cut time-to-hire by five days, and build the weekly pack ten-plus managers use to plan.

I work to strict GDPR standards on candidate data — every audit I've been part of has closed with zero compliance flags. I hold an MSc in Business Management from South East Technological University in Waterford and a Bachelor of Business Management from Osmania University. I'm based in Dublin, hold a full clean driver's licence and own transport, and I'm open to talent acquisition, recruitment and HR roles across Ireland.
